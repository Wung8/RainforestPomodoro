Features:
- procedural generation
- screen blocking during breaks
- music player

How To:
- click on Start and Stop Session to start and stop sessions
- click on the timer display to change time settings
- drag the ≡ icon on the top right of the grass to adjust ground level
- upload a folder with mp3 files into the playlist folder to add it as a playlist
- click on the gear icon to change settings
- settings:
  - music: music volume slider
  - rain: rain volume slider
  - scale: display scale slider
  - enable pausing: enables the pause button
  - enable titles: enables music title display
  - music on pause: enables music to play while session is paused
  - music on break: enables music to play while session is in break
  - enable blocking: enables Rainforest Pomodoro to block your screen during breaks
  - playlists: select playlist of mp3 files in playlists folder
